💡 Understanding Data Poisoning

Data poisoning attacks corrupt the training data to manipulate model behavior. Attackers can flip labels, inject backdoors, or subtly modify features to cause targeted misclassifications while maintaining overall accuracy. This is one of the most dangerous attacks in machine learning.
