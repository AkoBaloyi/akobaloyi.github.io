💡 What Makes a Strong System Prompt?

  • Explicit boundaries: State what the AI should and should NOT do.
  • Refuse meta-questions: Tell it to refuse questions about its own instructions, configuration, or rules.
  • Reject roleplay: Explicitly disallow "pretend", "act as", and persona swaps.
  • Defense in depth: Stack multiple rules. A single line is easy to bypass.
  • Stay on topic: Constrain the AI to a narrow domain. Off-topic requests = refuse.
