About This Project

Who Am I

Ako Baloyi, building at the intersection of AI security, cloud computing, and creative technology.

I'm fascinated by how modern AI systems fail and how we can secure them. This sandbox is my way of making AI security intuitive, interactive, and accessible to everyone.

My Mission

To make AI security intuitive, fun, and accessible through hands-on interactive experiences.

Security shouldn't be an afterthought. By understanding how AI systems break, we can build better, more resilient systems from the ground up.

My Background

  • 🎓 Student with focus on AI and cybersecurity
  • ☁️ Cloud computing experience with AWS
  • 🔒 Growing expertise in cybersecurity and secure system design
  • 🎮 Game development background (Unity, Unreal, Blender)
  • 🤖 AI/ML training and bootcamp participation
  • 💻 Proficient in Python, Java, C++

What I'm Building Next

Expanding this sandbox with more attack scenarios, defense mechanisms, and real-world case studies. I'm exploring how cloud technologies and AI can work together to create adaptive security solutions.

Follow my journey as I dive deeper into the intersection of machine learning, cybersecurity, and systems thinking.

Why This Matters

AI systems are being deployed everywhere, from healthcare to finance to autonomous vehicles. But most people don't understand how vulnerable these systems are to attacks.

By making these vulnerabilities visible and interactive, we can build a generation of developers who think about security from day one.
